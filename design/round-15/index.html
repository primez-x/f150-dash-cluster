F150 cluster · Design round 15 · Towing A with conditional focus

Towing A from round 10 is locked. Round 15 starts from round 14 and makes the screen follow the drive. One rule picks the reading that matters most right now: the focus. The focus shows numbers and size; every other secondary reading stays quiet. Speed, gear, both corner bands, and the fuel line never move. Pick A or B.

  • A · Focus slot: one fixed place on the right always holds the focus. A calm drive shows the quiet temperature bars there. Any other focus fills the slot with large values, and the other readings fold into a strip of symbols under the slot, tinted by state. The driver always looks at the same place.
  • B · Grow in place: every reading keeps the round 14 position. The focus grows and shows numbers in place; the other secondary readings dim.
  • The card question from rounds 12 to 14 stays open: both options keep the one-line card. In A the card stays bright; in B the card dims during a focus.
  • Numbers on a calm drive: speed, gear, the card value, and range, plus the clock and outside temperature.
