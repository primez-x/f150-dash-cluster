F150 cluster · Design round 25 · Towing A with two equal cards

Your direction: the four temperatures in one square card on the left, the speed in the center, the paged panel in a card of the same size on the right, the warning lights top right, the clock top left, numbers under the speed scale, and cruise and the drive mode under the numbers. The bottom-corner symbols now sit beside the corner ends of their bands. Every shot passes the margin check (capture.js: 26 shots).

  • Cards: 380 × 364 px each, at x 150 and x 1390, y 236 to 600. Both cards start under the top stacks, so six warning lights still fit above the right card.
  • Temperature card: transmission, coolant, oil, and differential, with taller bars. As on the locked Towing A, a value shows when that temperature leaves normal (and every value shows during a temperature focus).
  • Panel card: the same five pages (trailer, tires, engine, level, trip), with the values in one column.
  • Speed numbers every 20 mph under the scale, with MPH after 100. At crawl speed the steering bar replaces the scale, and the numbers hide.
  • Bottom corners: the rpm and boost symbols sit left of the band ends; the trailer brake symbol sits right of the brake band end, with BRAKING above the symbol.

Pick: open. A or B: where the gear goes.

  • A · The gear stays beside the speed digits; the row under the numbers holds cruise and the drive mode.
  • B · The gear joins the row under the numbers (cruise, gear, drive mode), so the speed digits stand alone on the center line.
