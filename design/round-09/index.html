F150 cluster · Design round 9 · Fuel bottom right

Fuel sits in the bottom-right corner on every layout, as on most clusters, so the driver finds fuel in one place in every mode. Builds on the round 8 hierarchy; the A and B options stay the same. Pick A or B for each layout from this round.

  • Fuel module: a band along the shroud with E at the corner and F toward the center, the level in the corner, and the range below the level. Towing B shows the same fuel module as a corner dial.
  • Daily layouts: instant economy moves to the bottom-left corner. Daily B option B keeps the temperature fan on the left.
  • Towing: RPM and boost stay bottom left. The trailer brake moves to the upper right, where the fuel bar sat: a segment bar with the gain as a grey range and a marker, lit in the accent colour while braking.
  • Low fuel turns the module amber, and the low-fuel light turns the module red, in the same corner on every layout.
