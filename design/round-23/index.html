F150 cluster · Design round 23 · Side stacks, compass top center, and cruise beside the speed

Round 23 replaces the round 22 top bar options with your direction: the warning lights stack on one side, the clock, date, and outside temperature stack on the other side, and the compass sits top center. Cruise moves left of the speed digits. Every mock draws the measured visible area and passes the 20 px margin check (capture.js: 40 shots, no content in a hidden area or the margin, no page errors).

  • Stacks: each stack hangs from y 62, between x 400 and 700 on the left or x 1220 and 1520 on the right, and aligns to the outer edge. The arch and the wheel rim hide everything further out.
  • Clock stack: the time, the date (Thu, Oct 30), and the outside temperature. On Towing the altitude joins the temperature line. Date field: rtcDate, the date part of the MCU clock (message 0x32), which the firmware sets from GPS time with the time zone.
  • Warning lights: rows that fill from the outer edge and grow downward, at 46 px (54 px before). Six wide lights fit in two rows (y 62 to 164).
  • Compass: the round 20 heading tape, 300 px wide, centered at the top. The tape is now on the Daily layouts too (field gpsBearing). The alert banner stays top center, under the tape.
  • Cruise: left of the speed digits, as far from the digits as the gear is on the right (Towing A: 119 px), level with the gear. Daily A has the gear under the digits, so cruise uses the same 119 px, at the gear height of Towing A. Daily B rings the digits with the speed arc, so cruise sits left of the gear row, outside the arc. The readout shows the symbol and the set speed only on every layout, as on the locked Towing A.

Picks: open. One letter for each layout:

  • A · Warning lights left, clock stack right.
  • B · Clock stack left, warning lights right.

The round 9 Daily picks and the round 20 level page pick from round 22 are still open; the round 23 pages show every one of those options with ?side=a|b.
