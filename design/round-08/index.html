F150 cluster · Design round 8 · One hierarchy

Superseded by round 9: fuel moves to the bottom-right corner on every layout. Pick A or B from round 9.

In round 7 too many elements competed for attention: bright accent bands in both corners, 104 px corner values, red and amber zones on every temperature gauge at rest, and the average economy in two places. Round 8 keeps the round 7 A and B options for each layout and applies one visual hierarchy to all six. The A or B pick for each layout now comes from this round.

  • Speed is the one hero. Gear stays large beside the speed.
  • The accent colour marks the primary gauges only: the speed and RPM scales, the RPM halo, and the RPM band. Live trailer braking also lights in the accent colour.
  • Secondary gauges (fuel, economy, boost, trailer brake gain, temperatures) fill in quiet grey with no glow. A low fuel level, a warm or hot temperature, or the reserve light still turns the gauge amber or red.
  • Temperature warning zones show as a faint tint at rest. The Daily B temperature fan shows no zone: the band colour carries the state. The RPM redline keeps its red band.
  • Corner values drop from 104 px to 80 px and from white to light grey. The corner sub line stays 24 px in grey. Read values (38 px) are dimmer than the speed.
  • Chrome is grey: gear number, card title, page dots. 2H, the normal drive state, shows as an outline chip; 4H, 4L, and the locker keep the filled chip.
  • Removed: the average economy in the corners (the card shows the average), the economy and trailer brake end labels, the center divider line on towing, and the repeated "TRAILER" label on the trailer card page (now "DISTANCE").

Corner use falls because the corner content is now dimmer, not smaller: the bands and values are the same, and the measure counts only bright pixels. The towing right corner also lost the 0 and 10 end labels. One constant, QUIET in kit8.js, sets the grey of the secondary bands if the corners read too dim on the truck.

Accent palettes

The accent setting changes only the primary gauges and live braking. Grey secondary gauges, amber, and red look the same with every accent. Round 8 lightens slate to 200, 212, 226: at the round 5 value, slate matched the quiet grey and the hierarchy disappeared. Add ?accent=teal, ice, blue, or slate to any round 8 page.
