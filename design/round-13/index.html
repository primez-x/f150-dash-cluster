F150 cluster · Design round 13 · Towing A with symbols and bars

Towing A from round 10 is locked. Rounds 12 and 13 replace text labels with symbols and gauge shapes, and align the screen into two groups that mirror each other across the center. Pick A or B.

Round 13 replaces the round 12 temperature rings with the vertical temperature bars. Every other round 12 change stays open for evaluation.

  • Both: the temperatures are four vertical bars. Each bar shows the value on top and the symbol under the bar. A warm or hot reading colours the bar, the value, and the symbol. The bars span the same width as the tire diagram group.
  • Both: symbols take the place of RPM, boost, TRAILER BRAKE, grade, and the temperature names. Temperatures show ° only. The fuel line at bottom center drops the E and F letters.
  • Both: new drawn symbols match the stock icon line weight: trailer, trailer brake, turbo, differential temperature, grade, tach, road, clock, average, instant economy, and service. The stock icons cover coolant, oil, transmission, battery, fuel, and range.
  • A · Bars and card: the paged card is one line with no title and no cell labels. A symbol names each value. The trailer page drops BRAKE TYPE (a vehicle setting).
  • B · Bars, no card: trailer distance joins the gear block, and the paged card is gone. Trip, economy, and vehicle pages are then not on the towing screen.
