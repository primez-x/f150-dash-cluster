F150 cluster · Design round 5 · Towing hybrid

Left half from towing A: speed, gear, RPM, and the paged card (alerts take the card position). Right half from towing B: temperature columns over tire pressures, fuel, and trailer brake. Builds on the round 4 kit. Daily A and Daily B both stay as separate layouts.

  • Trailer brake: the gain setting is always visible, with a marker on the output scale. While the brake pedal is pressed, the segments light up to the live output and the panel shows BRAKING.
  • Data gap: the firmware has the gain setting (trucksTrailerGain) but no field for the brake pedal or the live controller output. The live display needs a new vehicle signal. That work belongs to the vehicle protocol side, not the design.
  • Background glow: amber when any temperature is warm or fuel reaches 1/8 tank. Red when any temperature is hot or the truck low-fuel light comes on.
  • Turn signals: edge light only.

Accent colour setting

One setting colours every layout: Teal (default), Ice, Blue, or Slate. All layouts share the selected palette. Amber and red stay reserved for warnings, so warnings and the temperature glow look the same with every accent. Add ?accent=slate (or teal, ice, blue) to any round 4 or round 5 page to preview. On the cluster, the setting needs a saved accent value in Global.qml and a Display row in Settings. That work ships with the new layouts.
