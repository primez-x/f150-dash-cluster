F150 cluster · Design round 5 · Towing hybrid

Left half from towing A: speed, gear, RPM beside boost, and the paged card. Right half from towing B: temperature columns over tire pressures, fuel, and trailer brake. Builds on the round 4 kit. Daily A and Daily B both stay as separate layouts.

  • Trailer brake: the gain setting is always visible, with a marker on the output scale. While the brake pedal is pressed, the segments light up to the live output and the panel shows BRAKING.
  • Data gap: the firmware has the gain setting (trucksTrailerGain) but no field for the brake pedal or the live controller output. The live display needs a new vehicle signal. That work belongs to the vehicle protocol side, not the design.
  • Boost: manifold pressure in psi on one scale from 10 psi vacuum to 20 psi boost. The 2017+ 3.5 EcoBoost peaks near 16 psi. Boost fills right of the zero mark in the accent colour. Vacuum (closed throttle, as in the braking scene) fills left in quiet grey and reads as a negative value.
  • Data gap: the firmware has no boost or manifold pressure field. The gauge needs a new vehicle signal (vehicle protocol work).
  • Background glow: amber when any temperature is warm or the range drops to 50 miles. Red when any temperature is hot or the truck low-fuel light comes on.
  • Paged card: trailer (trailer miles, brake type), trip, fuel economy, and vehicle (oil pressure, oil life, battery). The trailer page shows first. Gain shows only on the trailer brake readout.
  • Alerts, quiet normal temperatures, no-data readings, fixed-width speed digits, night mode, and the shared redline: see the round 4 notes.
  • Usable area: the left stack, temperature columns, tires, fuel, and trailer brake all sit above the steering column shroud. See the round 4 notes.
  • Turn signals: edge light only.

Accent colour setting

One setting colours every layout: Teal (default), Ice, Blue, or Slate. All layouts share the selected palette. Amber and red stay reserved for warnings, so warnings and the temperature glow look the same with every accent. Add ?accent=slate (or teal, ice, blue) to any round 4 or round 5 page to preview. On the cluster, the setting needs a saved accent value in Global.qml and a Display row in Settings. That work ships with the new layouts.
