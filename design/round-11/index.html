F150 cluster · Design round 11 · Towing A clean-up

Towing A from round 10 is locked. Round 11 gives the locked layout two clean-up options. Pick A or B. Both options keep the corners and the fuel line at bottom center unchanged.

  • Both: grade joins the gear block under 2H, the divider beside the gear is gone, and the temperature labels lose the icons.
  • A · Grouped: every reading stays on the main screen. The temperature columns are shorter, and the tire block is smaller and sits under the temperatures.
  • B · Exceptions only: tire pressures move to card page 2 and return to the main screen only while a tire is low. The temperatures use short horizontal tracks. Under the temperatures the screen stays empty in normal driving.
