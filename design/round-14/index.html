F150 cluster · Design round 14 · Towing A with fewer numbers

Towing A from round 10 is locked. Round 14 starts from round 13 and removes numbers. Secondary readings show a number only when the reading needs attention. Pick A or B.

  • Both: temperature bars show no value at rest. A value appears over a bar when the reading is warm, hot, cold, or missing. The bars do not move when a value appears.
  • Both: the tire diagram is gone while every tire is normal. A low tire brings back the diagram with the low pressure only.
  • Both: the engine corner keeps the RPM band, the boost band, and the tach and turbo symbols, with no RPM or boost number.
  • Both: the fuel band keeps E and F and adds quarter marks, with a longer mark and a ½ label at the middle. The percent is gone; the range in miles stays.
  • Numbers at rest, A: speed, gear, grade, the card value, trailer brake gain, and range (with the clock and outside temperature: 19 numbers in round 13, 8 in round 14 A, 7 in round 14 B).
  • B: the same as A without the paged card and without trailer distance. Numbers at rest: speed, gear, grade, trailer brake gain, and range.
