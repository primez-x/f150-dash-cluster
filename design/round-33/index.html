F150 cluster · Design round 33 · Towing A · Engine gauge frame

The library audit found that JLY command 0x3B carries signed vacuum/boost, cylinder head temperature, and intake air temperature. The stock layouts never read these values. The menu-v7 data log records them; the truck log gives the units and scales. Every shot passes the margin check (capture.js: 16 shots).

  • Boost band: now vacuumBoost with vacuumBoostDir, one signed reading for vacuum and boost. The band keeps the round 4 psi scale until the log gives the raw unit.
  • Engine page: intake air temperature under rpm and boost. Intake air shows heat soak on a long pull.
  • Head temperature limits (warm 250 °F, hot 270 °F) are design values until checked against the truck.
  • Both options: a hot head temperature raises the red alert and the red glow, as a hot transmission does.

Pick: A, locked (2026-10-30). The head temperature takes a fifth column in the temperature card, between coolant and oil. A is the default; ?cht=b keeps option B for reference.
