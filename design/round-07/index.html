F150 cluster · Design round 7 · Full corners

Superseded by round 8: the same A and B options with one visual hierarchy. Pick A or B from round 8.

Round 6 put small readouts in the bottom corners and left most of the corner area empty. Round 7 gives each corner a real gauge. The wheel rim hides the top corners and the bottom corners stay visible, so the bottom corners now carry primary or secondary gauges. The clock and outside temperature go back to the top corners. Round 7 replaces the round 6 corner options; the round 6 pick is no longer needed.

  • A · Corner modules: a thick band along the shroud from the corner toward the center, and one large value (104 px) in the corner. The same module appears on every layout.
  • B · Main gauges move down: Daily A speed and RPM rise out of the corners as wings; Daily B temperatures fan out of the left corner; towing RPM and trailer brake become corner dials, as stock UI1 puts RPM and speed in the corners.
  • Normal states stay quiet: grey temperature bands, dim unlit segments. Amber and red appear only for a warning.
  • Add ?usable=1 to any layout to draw the corner area (orange) over the design.
  • Check from the driver seat that the bottom corners are visible through the wheel, and that the large values read at arm's length.
