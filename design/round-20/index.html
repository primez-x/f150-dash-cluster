F150 cluster · Design round 20 · Steering angle, roll, and heading on Towing A

Towing A is locked as round 19 option A (ruler). Round 20 adds the steering angle, the roll, and the compass heading. The firmware already carries these readings. None of them is verified on the truck yet.

Pick: open.

  • Crawl state: 8 mph and under, or in reverse. The speed scale carries nothing at crawl speed, so a steering bar takes the place of the scale. The fill runs from the center line to the wheel angle, and the knob is a tire seen from above. No number. Field: angle (getAngle(), MCU message 0x26 trackInfoNotify, the reverse camera guide lines).
  • Level page: at crawl speed the level page (grade and roll) takes the place of the engine page. Above crawl speed the engine page keeps the grade, as before. Fields: mPitch and mRoll, from the motion sensor inside the cluster.
  • Tilt focus: at crawl speed, a roll of 10° or more jumps the panel to the level page in amber; 20° or more shows red. The climb and descent focus also jump to the level page at crawl speed. The thresholds are design values, to tune on the truck.
  • Why crawl only: the motion sensor reads acceleration as tilt. A fast corner reads as roll, and hard braking reads as a descent. The firmware must also hold the tilt focus for a few seconds (hysteresis).
  • Compass: the heading in letters (8 points) beside the clock, in the quiet grey. Hidden at 5 mph and under, because the firmware stops updating the GPS bearing there.
  • A · Numbers: the grade and the roll as numbers with symbols, as on every other page. B · Truck outlines: a side view and a rear view of the truck at the true angle over a dashed level line, each with one number.
  • Not on Towing A: altitude (barometer and GPS), G-force, and the park sensors. Altitude and G-force suit an off-road layout. Ford turns off the rear park sensors with a trailer connected.

Truck checks (protocol work, separate from the design)

  • Does the MCU send 0x26 for this truck, and in every gear or only in reverse? The angle has no "no data" value, so a missing message reads as straight ahead. Check the sign (assumed: positive = right) and the full scale (45° each way).
  • Calibrate the motion sensor on level ground after install (mGyroCalibration). Check the roll sign (assumed: positive = right side down). 0xFFFF means no data and shows "--".
