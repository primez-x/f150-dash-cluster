Round 43 cluster sounds

Stock is the original firmware sound. A is the soft set. B is the crisp set. Active loudness matches stock within 1 dB for every file. Radar rows play the first 4 seconds of each loop. The stock radar players stop at 4 seconds too.

SoundUseStockA (soft)B (crisp)
direct_slow.wavTurn signal, normal flash. Three ticks at 0.00, 0.35 and 0.70 s (on, off, on).
direct_quick.wavTurn signal, fast flash (bulb out). Three ticks at 0.00, 0.11 and 0.31 s.
door.wavDoor ajar. One tone burst, 0 to 0.33 s. Information.
breaker.wavShort alert. One burst, 0 to 0.20 s. Warning.
ding.wavGeneral chime. One decaying chime, 1.2 s. Attention.
buzzer.wavUrgent warning. Continuous tone for the full 0.82 s.
seatbelt_slow.wavSeatbelt reminder, slow. Two pulsing voices that fade over 2.4 s.
seatbelt_quick.wavSeatbelt reminder, fast. The first 0.6 s of the slow pattern.
radar_b0.wavParking sensor, far. One beep every 0.48 s (4 s excerpt for A and B).
radar_b1.wavParking sensor. One beep every 0.35 s.
radar_b2.wavParking sensor. One beep every 0.27 s.
radar_b3.wavParking sensor. One beep every 0.18 s.
radar_b4.wavParking sensor, near. One beep every 0.14 s.
