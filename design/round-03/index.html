F150 cluster · Design round 3 · one fixed layout

Daily and Towing share one layout. Each item has one place in every mode, so the eye never searches. Sample values only.

  • Center: speed, gear, and drive state; fuel and range directly under the speed; rpm under fuel. One alert slot under rpm stays empty until something is wrong.
  • Left: transmission, coolant, and oil temperature. Right: tire pressures, then mode data (trip data in Daily, trailer gain and grade in Towing). Bottom: odometer, trip, and battery.
  • Top corners sit behind the wheel rim, so only the clock, mode name, and outside temperature go there. Telltales and cruise stay top center (off-road A contract).
  • Normal values are quiet grey. Caution is amber, warning is red, and both also fill the alert slot.
  • Turn signals: a green light along the screen edge with the stock arrow at mid height, bound to turnLeftLight / turnRightLight. The light fades with the flasher.
  • Text sizes: driving values 46 px, all other text at least 28 px. Basis: 13.1 in glass, model index 0 scale 0.7752, 750 mm eye distance, and the ISO 15008 character heights (about 20 and 12 arcmin).
