F150 cluster · Design round 6 · Bottom corners

The steering column shroud hides the bottom center of the screen. Above y 740 the full width is usable. Below y 740 the usable area narrows along the shroud into two corners. Each corner fits one tall readout, as stock UI1 uses the corners for RPM and km/h. A card or a three-cell row does not fit. Each layout below spends the corners on the room the shroud took away. Builds on the round 4 and round 5 layouts.

  • Corner readout: a small label over one large value, at the outer edge of the corner.
  • Shroud rail: a segment gauge that follows the shroud edge. The value fills from the corner toward the center.
  • Clock and outside temperature can move from the top corners, which the wheel rim hides, down to the bottom corners.
  • Check from the driver seat that the bottom corners are visible through the wheel before you pick.
